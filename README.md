# 📊 Business Sales Performance Analysis & Decision Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing business sales performance using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven management decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare regional sales performance
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Evaluate product category performance
- Identify important business insights
- Support data-driven business decisions

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Pivot Tables
- Data Visualization
- Business Analytics

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Average Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Best Performing Region
- Top Performing Product

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Interactive Region Filter
- Product Category Filter
- Product Filter
- Date Filter
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top Products Analysis
- Region-wise Sales Analysis
- Product-level Analysis
- Sales and Profit Analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

- North is the best-performing region.
- Office Chair is the top-performing product.
- Total revenue is approximately ₹6.85 million.
- Total profit is approximately ₹1.14 million.
- Average profit margin is approximately 16.7%.
- Sales performance varies across different months.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus inventory planning on high-performing products.
- Analyze lower-margin products for pricing optimization.
- Strengthen sales strategies in weaker-performing regions.
- Monitor monthly sales fluctuations.
- Use product-level profitability analysis to support business decisions.
